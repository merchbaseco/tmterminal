# What this is

Trademark Terminal is a searchable United States trademark service for print-on-demand sellers. It reads USPTO source files as they are published and keeps live trademark tables current. You search, screen listing copy, and open a mark. You do not get a legal verdict.

Use it when you want evidence before you print a word on a shirt. Do not use it as a substitute for the USPTO file or counsel.

## What you can do

- Search a word mark with Multi, Split, or Wildcard.
- Paste listing copy and see live marks that appear as exact phrases.
- Screen one phrase per line and get live exact and live partial counts.
- Open a mark by its eight-digit serial number.
- Call the same jobs from the CLI, the TypeScript client, or hosted MCP.

## What you cannot do

- Treat a zero as “safe.” Counts are evidence.
- Search by fuzzy guess at a serial or registration number. Those are identities.
- See every international class. V1 materializes International Class 025.
- Repair source files or inspect operator errors. That is not a seller surface.
